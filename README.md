# NX-08

**NX-08** is a psychological sci-fi book where reality, identity, and technology continuously overlap. The story unfolds through classified logs, sealed transmissions, and field reports that document an ongoing conflict involving humans, unknown entities, and hidden infrastructures capable of influencing memory, perception, and consciousness.

Inspired by works such as *Serial Experiments Lain*, NX-08 blends cyberpunk, artificial intelligence, networks, existential horror, and mystery into an interconnected narrative. Every post reveals another fragment of a larger story, inviting readers to piece together the truth behind the project.

The book was first written on Sat, Jan 17.
Published online on Fri, Jul 31.

Created and published in Brazil.
